How to read the result

  • The “before blocking” line is the free win. It is the traffic you are paying for today without using it. Closing that gap costs engineering time once and reduces every future invoice.
  • Retries are already inside the attempts figure. A 90% pool needs about 11% more requests than you counted. A 70% pool needs 43% more — that is the hidden tax on a cheap pool.
  • Your time is a real cost line. Set your rate honestly. A pool that needs two extra hours a month is not cheap at any per-GB price.
  • Per-IP does not pay for failures in traffic, but it does not give you unlimited concurrency either — you rent addresses, and that is your ceiling.

Where the inputs come from

Page weight: measure it, do not guess — most browsers show transferred size in the network panel. Success rate: take it from a real test on your own URLs, as described in how to test a proxy before you buy it. Prices: from your vendor's current page; we deliberately do not quote vendor prices here because they change. If you are comparing several address classes at once, providers that sell datacenter, ISP and residential under one account — as IPRoyal does — let you read the real numbers from a single dashboard instead of estimating.

Estimates only. The tool makes no network requests: everything is computed in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
