What this site is

ProxyStack publishes hands-on notes about the infrastructure side of data collection: proxies, browser automation, remote machines and the tooling around them. The aim is narrow — help you pick the right setup once, instead of re-buying it three times.

  • We describe what a tool is wrong for, not only what it is good at.
  • We do not publish invented prices or limits. Where a figure is uncertain, we link to the vendor's own pricing page and say so.
  • Affiliate relationships are disclosed on every page where they exist and never decide placement.

Some links on this site are monetised. They carry rel="sponsored" and never affect what is recommended.
